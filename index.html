Pinterest Taste Library

A personal, single-user tool built and operated by its owner.

What it does

Pinterest Taste Library is a small application that runs on its owner's own computer. It connects to the owner's Pinterest account through the official Pinterest API and reads the boards and pins that the owner has saved. Each saved image is then labelled locally with a category, descriptive tags and style notes, so the owner can browse, search and analyse their own design preferences over time.

  • Used by one person only: the Pinterest account owner. There are no other users and no public access.
  • Read-only. It requests the boards:read, pins:read and user_accounts:read scopes and never creates, edits, deletes or publishes pins, boards or comments.
  • Runs periodically to pick up newly saved pins.
  • Not a social network, marketplace or content-distribution service.

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026.

  • What is collected. Board names, pin titles, descriptions, links, creation dates and the pin images from the owner's own Pinterest account, plus the OAuth tokens needed to read them.
  • Where it is stored. Only on the owner's own computer, in a local database and image folder. Nothing is uploaded to a public server or shared with other people.
  • Third parties. Images are sent to an AI image-labelling service (OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to) solely to generate descriptive tags. No other data is shared, and no data is sold.
  • Retention and deletion. Data stays until the owner deletes the local data folder. Pinterest access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's settings under Security → Connected apps, which immediately stops all access.
  • Children. The tool is not intended for or used by anyone under 18.
  • Contact. Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner at the email address shown on this site's repository.
